One-line scope: After the demerger, Allcargo is a ₹1,500 Cr domestic express and contract-logistics business. The stock is −26% from its August high, below its 200-DMA, with volume dried up. Wait for the 9.30 cluster.

Red flags:

  • PAT included a ₹8 Cr one-off lease-closure gain. Without it, PAT was about ₹6 Cr.
  • The auditor flagged that the scheme's accounting overrides Ind AS, though its conclusion was unmodified.
  • Founder Shashi Kiran Shetty resigned as chairman on 5 Aug.
  • The promoter stake fell from 63.3% to 40.3%, mostly from the scheme.
  • FII (8.2 → 6.6%) and DII (3.2 → 1.8%) holdings are both falling.
  • There is a ₹5.6 Cr tax demand tied to the demerged business.

Guidance read: The express margin path (6.2 → 7.5 → 10%) is the only guidance. It is very sensitive to diesel. A peace-driven crude fall helps Allcargo more than almost any name in the book.
