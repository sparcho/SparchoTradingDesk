The one sentence that matters: this is an operationally sound power business (19% margins,

positive operating cash) sitting inside a broken capital structure. The equity is a **residual claim

behind ₹6,542 Cr of debt on a negative book** — which is exactly why the share price is so violent in

both directions. Equity in a levered, negative-net-worth entity behaves like an option, not like a

shareholding.

Why this reframes the position

The operator's 30-Jul structural read already flagged VEDPOWER as the cut candidate, on a purely

technical basis: breakeven at 40.02 sits above every mapped resistance (ceiling 39.56), so the

position could not return to cost without breaking out of its entire structure. That was correct and

it was chart-derived.

This brief adds the reason the chart looks like that. **A stock whose equity sits behind 3.4× debt on

a negative book does not mean-revert to your cost basis because you would like it to.** The −16%

realised on today's 4,250 shares is not bad luck; it is what a levered residual claim does when the

leverage does not resolve.

**The residual 3,000 shares (₹1,03,560 at the 34.52 close, ~11.5% of the reconciled book) is a

position in a company with negative net worth.** That is a materially different statement from

"VEDPOWER is annoying me", and it deserves a deliberate decision rather than an emotional one.

What would have to be true for the bull case

Stated honestly, because a fail-list is not an analysis:

  1. Deleveraging. The gap between ₹1,080 Cr operating profit and −₹1,713 Cr net is financing and

non-cash charges. A refinancing at a lower rate, a promoter infusion, or an asset sale that

retires debt changes the arithmetic fast — ICR 1.78 → 3×+ transforms the equity.

  1. The operating line holding. 19% OPM and +₹556 Cr CFO are the assets here. If margins hold

while debt shrinks, equity re-rates hard because it is levered — the same gearing that hurt.

  1. Promoter action. 56.38% held means the promoter's own stake is impaired too; that aligns

incentives toward a fix. It is not a guarantee of one.

None of these are chart-visible. They arrive as announcements. Which means the correct way to

hold this name — if it is held — is against a news trigger, not a price trigger.
