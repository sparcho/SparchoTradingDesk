7. "Bank-led QE" — the plain version + the operator's mechanics questions

Normal QE = the Fed prints and buys bonds (visible, politically toxic at 4% inflation). Bank-led QE = loosen the SLR (the eSLR buffer cut, ~5-6%→3.5-4.25%, live 2026-04-01) so COMMERCIAL BANKS can absorb the issuance instead → same effect (giant buyer soaks up bonds, yields held down, liquidity flood) WITHOUT the Fed visibly printing = "QE laundered through the banks." Dollar debases anyway. (Video said "restore SLR exemption"; actual = the milder buffer cut — regulators declined the outright exemption.) Fragile because the trade needs a STEEP curve; the market keeps handing a FLAT one.

Operator's mechanics questions (answered honestly, speculation flagged):

  • When do bonds get sold / why buyers take them? They mostly MATURE (repaid) and get refinanced with new issuance — not "sold." Fed holds to maturity or runs off (=QT); banks hold as collateral/HQLA or sell in the deep secondary market. Buyers take them for yield + safety + they're the base COLLATERAL of the whole system (repo/margin/liquidity rules). Always a bid — the question is at what YIELD; kill artificial demand and yields rise until private buyers clear = bigger gov interest bill = the debt-spiral fear.
  • Banks hit their limit → dump the dollar? Conflation fix: buying TREASURIES ≠ buying "DXY" (Treasuries are dollar assets, so it's marginally dollar-supportive, but a domestic balance-sheet op, not an FX allocation choice). At capacity the marginal-buyer problem returns → Fed does overt QE (debasement accelerates, visible) OR yields rise OR the rule loosens again. Bank-led QE buys TIME, not infinity. The ones who diversify OUT of dollars are FOREIGN central banks (slowly, into gold) — that's a gold bid, not domestic banks.
  • Shotgun credit crises? YES, and it's mainstream not conspiracy: the pivot to cut+QE historically needs a CATALYST that gives cover (2008 GFC, 2019 repo, 2020 COVID, 2023 SVB). The road to the endgame is PUNCTUATED by funding/credit accidents, each forcing more liquidity → "Branch B is the path to Branch A."
  • Curve flattens after banks load up? = the SVB-March-2023 template exactly. Banks buy long funded short; flatten/invert → negative carry + mark-to-market losses if yields rise (SVB's hole) → banks stop/sell → yields spike → bank stress → Fed backstop (BTFP) → mini-QE → pivot via crisis. Already happened in miniature.
  • Where "conspiracy" starts: every MECHANISM above is real/evidenced (SVB, BTFP, eSLR reform, CB gold buying, fiscal dominance). The SPECULATIVE part = the INTENT (a deliberate Warsh master-plan). June minutes give it zero support. Trust the plumbing, hold the "grand design" loosely.
