L1 — 2-sentence summary

The Warsh Fed is running "hawkish now, dovish later": it banks anti-inflation credibility by holding rates and dropping forward guidance, while assembling the conditions (oil down + a trimmed-mean-PCE framing + freed bank balance-sheet capacity) to justify later cuts/QE that debase the dollar. For the INR book the only question that matters is which branch we are on — orderly (deal -> soft data -> cut -> QE; dollar down/gold up) vs disorderly (new-chair drawdown; dollar up/stocks down/yields up/gold down) — and the US curve shape plus a 5-asset scorecard tell us in real time.

L2 — The verified thesis (what is actually real)

FOMC 2026-06-17 (Warsh's first, verified): unanimous 12-0 hold at 3.50-3.75% (4th straight hold); 9 of 18 dots now pencil >=1 hike in 2026 (6 see multiple); 17 of 18 judge inflation risk tilted up; the median flipped to a hike; Warsh abstained from his own dot; communications overhaul + task forces announced; statement cut to ~130 words.

The mechanism stack (the "data machine"):

  1. Credibility bank — look tough on inflation now so later cuts read as data-driven, not political (Trump "I love inflation"). Independence theatre.
  2. Trimmed-mean PCE — real Dallas Fed gauge (asymmetric trim, 24% top / 31% bottom) that strips volatile outliers; verified as Warsh's preferred measure (CNN, Brookings, Jun-2026). The lever to reframe a CPI-worst-in-3-years print as "underlying inflation is fine."
  3. eSLR buffer cut (CORRECTED — not a Treasury "exemption") — the 2025 enhanced-SLR reform RECALIBRATES the GSIB buffer (from 5%/6% down to ~3.5-4.25%), final rule effective 2026-04-01. Regulators explicitly declined to adopt the outright Treasury exemption. Net effect still frees bank balance sheet to absorb issuance = "QE laundered through commercial banks" without money-printing optics — but the mechanism is a buffer cut, not a carve-out.
  4. Fragile marginal buyer — Cayman basis-trade hedge funds absorbed 37% of net Treasury issuance Jan-2022->Dec-2024 (verified, Fed/Finadium); holdings +$1T to $1.85T, now the largest foreign holder via repo-financed basis trades = margin-call fragility if repo/vol spikes.

Why it is fragile: the plan needs a STEEPENER (banks profit from a steep curve on the SLR trade); the decision printed a FLATTENER. New-chair drawdown bias is real (Barclays: 1mo -5%, 3mo -12%, 6mo -16%; Greenspan -33% on Black Monday). The old stocks-down/bonds-up hedge is broken (decision day: stocks down AND yields up).

L3 — The two-branch scenario tree

Branch A — ORDERLY (plan works). Iran deal holds -> oil falls -> trimmed-mean PCE softens -> Warsh cuts + restores QE-via-banks -> dollar down, gold up, curve STEEPENS. INR read: USDINR eases, imported-inflation relief, risk-on for rate-sensitives/financials.

Branch B — DISORDERLY (plan fails). Deal breaks / Hormuz oil spike OR bond-market disbelief -> yields up + stocks down + dollar up + gold down -> new-chair drawdown into Oct-Nov (the -16%/6mo window). INR read: USDINR pressure, FII outflow — the YELLOW / QUICK_EXIT regime already running.

Current lean: Branch B-leaning. A flattener printed, the deal has not held, the FOMC was hawkish. This is precisely why TNX is the binding regime gate and we sit YELLOW 58 (see REGIME_SCORING.md). (Reaffirmed 2026-07-09 — see update below.)
