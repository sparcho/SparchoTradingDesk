Why it might fail — the watch signals (most useful part for us)

  • Bond market disbelief / wrong curve shape. On the decision the curve flattened (2Y up to ~4.2%, 30Y barely moved) — the opposite of the steepener the plan needs (banks need a steep curve to profit from the SLR trade). Dropping forward guidance adds a risk premium.
  • Refinancing wall + fragile buyer base. ~$8T US debt to refinance in the next year; foreign central banks stopped buying since ~2014; leveraged hedge funds (Cayman) bought ~37% of new issuance since 2022 → margin-call fragility. The old stocks-down/bonds-up safety net is broken — Wednesday saw stocks down AND yields up simultaneously.
  • Oil is the X-factor. Needs the Iran deal to hold AND oil down to get the disinflation excuse — but the deal hasn't held, ~500 tankers are stuck, and analysts say 2–3 months to normalize traffic even if signed.
  • New-chair drawdown history (Barclays/Bloomberg): S&P averages ~−12% in the first 3 months of a new Fed chair; big regime-changers far worse (Greenspan ~−33%); continuity chairs (Powell/Yellen/Bernanke) milder. Warsh = big change → drawdown risk into Oct–Nov, unless they thread the needle (deal done → soft data → cut → QE).
