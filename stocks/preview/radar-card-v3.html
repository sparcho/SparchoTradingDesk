Fewer cards, each one able to say why

You said the Radar showed a lot of hero cards and felt structureless — that it was not refining the fires using signals we are confident about. Both halves were true. Every fire drew a full card, and the ORDER came from the confluence score, which is ladder count plus moving-average hits plus timeframe stacking. All three were measured on 18-Aug against a fair control and not one of them predicts anything. The desk was shouting loudest about the part it had already disproved.

And re-ordering it turned up something worse
Two of the seven fires were telling you to buy at a price the stock is nowhere near.
A FIRE is supposed to mean price is sitting ON a support you verified. The gate checked that price was at some confluence, then took the buy level from the strongest support zone anywhere below — and nothing tied those two together. So STLTECH fired at Rs 627.15 saying buy at Rs 315.07, 99% below, and ASTRAMICRO at Rs 1,662.20 saying buy at Rs 1,145.75, 45% below — both under the sentence “price has come back to this level”. The reward-for-risk inherits the error, which is why STLTECH read 9.76× and sorted to the top the moment I ordered by it. Fourteen names carried an unreachable level in total. They are now demoted to Watch with the distance stated, not deleted — sound structure, price simply not there yet. Fires today: 5, not 7.
The rule
A card gets hero weight only when something measured backs it.
Two entry conditions have ever beaten a fair control, out of twenty-five tested. A fire that currently matches one of them leads. Every other fire is still here, complete and tradeable — it just stops presenting itself as a headline.
