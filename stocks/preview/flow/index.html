⚡ Queue for the watchdog
Watchdog queue — the option you picked. The button writes a request into a queue the watchdog reads on its next run. Known classes it heals with no session at all; anything it cannot heal it writes into the next handover with full context, so the fix is the first thing picked up rather than something you have to remember and retype.

And the watchdog IS alive — it wrote today's health report at 20:56. But three of its sibling jobs (Health Verify, Morning Establish, Morning Roll) were being killed mid-run by the same battery-stop setting that killed PULSE this morning. All three are fixed. Your instinct to ask was right.

The one honest limit: a page on GitHub Pages cannot write to your laptop, so the queue goes through the repo — the button commits the request, the watchdog pulls it. That means the round trip is as fast as the watchdog's next run, not instant.
